Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

My Colorful Monsters (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a children's creative brand featuring friendly monster characters, coloring pages, activities, books, and media. This policy describes the current practices of the website at mycolorfulmonsters.com.

Information you provide

At this time, this website does not offer visitor accounts, contact forms, comment features, or an email newsletter signup. We do not ask visitors to submit their names, email addresses, or other personal information through the website.

Information processed by website hosting and third-party services

Although we do not currently ask visitors to submit personal information, visiting a website can involve technical information being processed by the hosting provider or services used to display content. These providers may receive information such as an IP address, browser or device information, and the page or resource requested, as described in their own policies.

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. It also loads some images and audio from GitHub-hosted files, uses Google Fonts, links to Amazon for the coloring book, and embeds a YouTube video using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain. When you access those resources or follow those links, the relevant provider may process information under its own privacy practices. We do not control those providers' data practices.

Analytics

We have not installed Google Analytics or another website analytics tool on this website. If we decide to add analytics or other tracking in the future, we will review applicable requirements and update this policy to explain the relevant practices before enabling it.

Children's privacy

My Colorful Monsters is a family-friendly brand with content that may appeal to children. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through this website. If the website's features or data practices change, we will review applicable children's privacy requirements and make appropriate updates.

Parents or guardians who have a question or concern about privacy may contact us using the email address below.

Links to other websites

This website contains links to third-party websites and services. Their privacy policies and practices apply when you visit or use those services. Please review their policies for more information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when changes are made.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact mycolorfulmonsters@gmail.com.

Note: This policy describes the website's current intended practices and is not legal advice. Third-party services may process technical information when their content is loaded. We will review this policy if we add analytics, forms, signups, or other features.
