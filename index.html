Meet the Monster Crew

Eight kinds of friendly characters, each ready to join your next coloring adventure. Hover over a monster—or tap it on a touchscreen—to hear its sound!

Little Pond Monster

Little Pond Monster

Little Pond Monster is a sweet, quiet little monster who takes his time warming up to new friends. Once he feels comfortable, his playful and silly side shines! He loves spending time with familiar friends but enjoys quiet moments on his own, too.

Tap or select to see the character

Little Zombie

Little Zombie

Little Zombie is the gentle sweetheart of the group! Though he doesn't speak, he shares his feelings through gestures, expressions, and drawings. His playful antics bring joy, and his friends learn to understand him and trust the kindness behind everything he does.

Tap or select to see the character

Little Scarecrow

Little Scarecrow

Little Scarecrow loves helping his friends, building useful things, and solving problems in creative ways. He believes in teamwork and is always ready to listen, offer comfort, and help a friend who feels sad or left out.

Tap or select to see the character

Little Skeleton

Little Skeleton

Little Skeleton is a laid-back daydreamer who quietly observes the world around him. Calm, thoughtful, and subtly funny, he notices little details that others miss. He may not say much, but when he does, his words often bring a clever observation, a helpful clue, or an unexpected laugh.

Tap or select to see the character

Little Vampire

Little Vampire

Little Vampire is a confident, charming showman who loves being the center of attention! With grand entrances, fancy poses, witty remarks, and dramatic gestures, he turns even everyday moments into a performance. To him, life is always a chance to put on a show!

Tap or select to see the character

Little Werewolf

Little Werewolf

Little Werewolf is a brave, loyal, and protective friend who always looks out for others. He stands up for anyone being treated unfairly, even when it feels difficult. He knows that being brave doesn't mean never feeling scared—it means doing what's right even when you are.

Tap or select to see the character

Little Witch

Little Witch

Little Witch is an imaginative dreamer full of stories, spells, and possibilities. She can turn an ordinary walk into a magical adventure or a simple object into a new idea. She loves exploring her imagination and sharing it with friends.

Tap or select to see the character

Little Furry

Little Furry

Little Furry is a goofy, energetic monster who loves making everyone laugh! Whether he’s dancing, making silly faces, or trying his next funny idea, he’s always ready for fun. Beneath all that goofiness, he has a big heart and cares deeply about his friends.

Tap or select to see the character
